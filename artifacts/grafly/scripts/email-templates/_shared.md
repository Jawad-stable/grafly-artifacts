# Shared design tokens for the Grafly email templates

All 6 templates share the same visual language so users instantly recognize a
Grafly email regardless of why we're contacting them.

- **Cyan**  `#00A4FA` — primary CTA, accents
- **Lime**  `#E3ED43` — eyebrow / badge highlights
- **Pink**  `#FF7BD0` — secondary accent
- **Navy**  `#21263F` — body text on light bg, dark surface bg
- **Off-white** `#F5F6FA` — page background
- Font stack: `'Nunito', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- Email-safe: tables, inline styles, no JS, max width 560px, dark-mode aware via `prefers-color-scheme`.
